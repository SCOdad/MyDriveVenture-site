Drive. Log. Level up.

The driving stays focused on the road. The game begins when you’re parked.

  1. 01

    Practice together

    Head out with your grown-up. Build experience on different roads, in different conditions, at your own pace.

  2. 02

    Save the drive

    Once safely parked, log the start and end time. Drive Venture calculates daytime and nighttime minutes.

  3. 03

    See what adds up

    Watch practice become Road XP, quests, and milestones. Use the cockpit to find your next useful practice goal.

No play while driving. Drive Venture rewards completed practice and varied experience—never speed, risky maneuvers, or phone use behind the wheel.
