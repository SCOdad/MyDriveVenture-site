How it works

After you join

Drive first. Game after the car is parked.

Drive Venture is built around supervised practice—not phone use behind the wheel. After the drive, families can log the trip, track progress, and see which kinds of practice may be useful next.

01

Practice together

Drive with a qualified grown-up in real traffic, road, weather, and lighting conditions.

02

Log the drive

Save practice through the web or Text Parker after the drive is over.

03

Watch progress grow

See hours, night driving, milestones, quests, and Road XP build over time.

No play while driving. Drive Venture rewards completed practice and progress—never speed, risky maneuvers, or phone use behind the wheel.

Drive Venture is currently accepting pilot families in Michigan and Kansas. Licensing rules differ by state; always confirm your current official requirements.

Drive Venture is not a driving school and does not replace parental supervision, professional instruction, or government-required records.
