A growing journey

Your roads. More possibilities.

Drive logging is available nationwide. Michigan and Kansas currently have supported state-specific licensing rulesets. In other states, Drive Venture uses general practice goals rather than legal licensing requirements.

More state rulesets will follow, prioritized by where Drive Venture families are.

Always confirm current requirements with your state. Drive Venture supports practice; it does not replace instruction, supervision, or official licensing records.
