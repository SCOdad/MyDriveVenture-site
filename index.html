Supervised driving, made more engaging

Learning to drive is a journey.

Drive Venture helps families track supervised driving practice, understand licensing progress, and make the road to independent driving more engaging.

Ask about the pilot

Track practice

Families record supervised drives and maintain a readable history of practice time.

See progress

Drive Venture helps families understand progress toward state licensing requirements.

Enjoy the ride

Quests, milestones, and Parker—the Drive Venture guide—add encouragement without distracting from safety.

Currently in pilot

Drive Venture is an early pilot built around Google Forms, Google Sheets, and family-supervised practice. Participation is limited and may change as we learn from pilot families.

Drive Venture is not a driving school and does not replace parental supervision, professional instruction, official licensing requirements, or records required by a government agency.
