Why Drive Venture exists

Fifty hours is a long road. The goal is more than reaching a number.

Many states require supervised practice before a teen can earn a license. Those hours are an opportunity to build judgment, confidence, and experience while a grown-up is there to coach. Drive Venture helps families make that practice visible, varied, and easier to complete together.

When to use it

From learner’s permit to the next license milestone.

Start when supervised practice begins. Keep logging through daylight, darkness, familiar routes, unfamiliar roads, and the full set of hours your licensing pathway requires.

Starting in Michigan: Drive Venture currently supports families working toward Michigan’s 50 supervised hours, including 10 hours at night.

Built for families

Starting in Michigan. Built for every road.

Drive Venture starts in Michigan, where pilot families are working toward 50 supervised hours, including 10 at night. We are building a simple way to keep those hours visible, share the work, and learn what helps families practice together.

Drive Venture is operated by Michael Stefaniak as a Michigan sole proprietor. It is not a driving school and does not replace parental supervision, professional instruction, official licensing requirements, or records required by a government agency.

Why all that practice?

Practice helps teens get ready.

New drivers are more likely to crash when they first drive alone. Practice with a grown-up gives teens time to learn on real roads, make safe choices, and get help when they need it.

01

More practice can help

A large 2025 U.S. study found fewer crashes and close calls for teens who got more supervised practice before driving on their own. Source and evidence context →

02

Try many kinds of drives

Daytime is only one kind of drive. Night, rain, traffic, new roads, and longer trips help teens learn what the road can be like.

03

A log shows what to try next

A driving log helps families see what they have done and what they can practice next, like night driving or busy roads.

What the study tells us: More supervised practice was linked with fewer crashes and close calls later. It does not promise a result, but it is a strong reason to make practice a family habit.
