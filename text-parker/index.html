Planned SMS service

Text Parker

Text Parker is not yet available. The number has been reserved, but enrollment and drive logging will not begin until compliance review, testing, and implementation are complete.

Text Parker is a planned optional way for Drive Venture families to begin enrollment and record supervised practice drives by text message using (320) 372-7537, branded as (320) 3-PARKER.

Planned commands

  • JOINBegin the Drive Venture registration or enrollment process.
  • STARTStart recording a supervised practice drive.
  • DONEFinish the current practice drive.
  • HELPReceive assistance and contact information.
  • STOPStop receiving Drive Venture text messages.

Safety comes first

After a driver sends START, Parker will confirm that the drive has begun, tell the driver to put the phone away, and ask the driver to respond only after safely parking.

Do not read, type, or interact with a phone while driving. A supervising adult should manage any necessary communication.

SMS disclosures

  • Text Parker participation is voluntary and is not required to use Drive Venture.
  • Messages are transactional and relate to enrollment, supervised-drive recording, service status, and requested help.
  • Message frequency varies based on use.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email mike@mydriveventure.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
