Get started with Parker

Text JOIN to (320) 372-7537

Or remember it as (320) 3-PARKER.

Open a text with JOIN

By texting JOIN, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Drive Venture about registration, your Drive Venture account, and driving activity. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Privacy Policy • Terms of Service

Text Parker is a planned optional service for Drive Venture families. Messages are transactional—not unsolicited marketing—and will support registration, account activity, supervised-drive recording, service status, and requested help.

How the commands work

  • JOINRegister, get started, and opt in to Text Parker.
  • STARTBegin a supervised driving session.
  • DONEFinish the current supervised driving session.
  • HELPGet help and Drive Venture contact information.
  • STOPOpt out and stop receiving Drive Venture text messages.

Safety comes first

After a driver sends START, Parker will confirm that the drive has begun, tell the driver to put the phone away, and ask the driver to respond only after safely parking.

Do not read, type, or interact with a phone while driving. A supervising adult should manage any necessary communication.
