Optional SMS consent
I agree to receive transactional text messages from Drive Venture through Text Parker at the mobile number provided. Messages may include enrollment assistance, supervised-drive start and finish confirmations, service updates, and requested help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not required to use Drive Venture. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

The checkbox shown above is intentionally empty. In the production Form, the respondent must actively select it.

Enrollment rules

  • SMS participation is optional.
  • The consent control is separate and unchecked by default.
  • A family may complete Drive Venture enrollment without SMS consent.
  • Each recipient consents for their own mobile number.
  • A parent or guardian authorizes participation for a driver under 18.
  • The submitted phone number and consent record are retained with the policy version and timestamp.
