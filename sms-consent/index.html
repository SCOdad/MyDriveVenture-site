Text Parker SMS consent

Production enrollment: The Drive Venture Michigan pilot onboarding page at mydriveventure.com/join contains the live SMS opt-in controls described below.

Drive Venture treats each person's mobile number and Text Parker consent separately. The current Join form collects only grown-up acquisition information; it does not ask for mobile numbers or SMS permission. Text Parker is optional and requires its own affirmative opt-in.

Grown-up consent

Optional: Text Parker for the grown-up
I agree to receive transactional text messages from Drive Venture through Text Parker at the mobile number provided. Messages may include enrollment assistance, supervised-drive start and finish confirmations, service updates, and requested help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not required to use Drive Venture. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Driver consent

Optional: Text Parker for the driver
The driver may agree to receive transactional Drive Venture text messages at the driver mobile number provided. Messages may include enrollment, drive start/finish interactions, progress messages, and requested help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not required to use Drive Venture. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

The boxes on this page are illustrative examples of voluntary Text Parker consent language, not live enrollment controls. The current Join form does not collect SMS consent. People may opt in for their own number through an approved Text Parker enrollment path, including sending JOIN from that phone.

Enrollment and recordkeeping rules

  • SMS participation is optional and is not a condition of Drive Venture participation.
  • Consent is specific to the participating person and mobile endpoint.
  • Creating a Family, entering a number, or making a purchase never automatically opts someone into SMS. An approved separate affirmative action is required.
  • Reply STOP opts the recipient out and HELP requests help.
  • Submitted mobile numbers are stored separately for the associated people.
  • Affirmative SMS consent is recorded with the consent-text version and timestamp in the Drive Venture service.
  • A parent or guardian authorizes pilot participation for a driver under 18.
