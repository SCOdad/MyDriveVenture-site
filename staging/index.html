The road we are building

Fifty hours is a long road. We help families travel it together.

Many states require teens to complete supervised practice before earning a license. Drive Venture helps teens and their grown-ups record that experience, understand what remains, and make room for the varied practice that builds confidence behind the wheel.

Starting in Michigan: Drive Venture currently supports families working toward Michigan’s 50 supervised hours, including 10 hours at night.

Why all that practice?

The number is a benchmark. The experience is the point.

Fifty hours is a common policy target, not a magic threshold. Supervised practice gives a new driver time to build familiarity, encounter different conditions, and see where more experience is still needed.

01

Build familiarity

Repeated practice supports smoother vehicle control, observation, judgment, and hazard recognition.

02

Add variety

Daylight is only one route. Weather, darkness, traffic, road types, and unfamiliar trips all add useful experience.

03

See what remains

A clear log helps families spot gaps instead of discovering them near the licensing deadline.
