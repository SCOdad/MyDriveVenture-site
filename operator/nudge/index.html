What would Drive Venture send next?

Weekly Certification is a fixed system communication scheduled for Monday at 3 PM local. Add-first-driver activation is evaluated daily at 3 PM local, beginning the calendar day after registration. First-drive activation begins the first Thursday strictly after driver creation; other lifecycle nudges stay on Thursday at 3 PM local. Lifecycle sending uses a rolling seven-day allowance per recipient and driver, with an independent family-level allowance. Selected means eligible for its scheduled day; delivery still requires the 3 PM local window and an active master control; suppressed means blocked by a guardrail such as an already-used weekly slot.
