Logging practice drives

Understand what to record during supervised practice, and how Drive Venture turns completed drives into progress.

Learn · Requirements · Getting Started · Practice · Logging · Research

What to record

For each supervised practice drive, keep the date, duration, supervisor and notes about the experience. Where relevant, distinguish daytime and nighttime practice and record conditions that will help plan future sessions.

What counts legally?

States differ in required totals, definitions, record formats and whether electronic logs are accepted. Drive Venture helps track practice but does not replace any mandated official log, certification or supervising-adult signature. Check official state sources.

Log only when parked

Use the web driving log or optional Text Parker workflow after parking. Never use a phone while operating a vehicle.

Review your progress

Logged hours, night driving and completed practice can help a family notice gaps and choose the next useful experience. XP and quests reward completed practice, not speed or risky maneuvers.
