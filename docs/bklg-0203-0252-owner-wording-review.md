## Terms & Conditions

Terms & Conditions
A few ground rules before the next drive.
We know: nobody comes to Drive Venture for the Terms & Conditions. But when families are recording driving hours and trusting us with personal information, it's worth being clear about what the service does, what you're responsible for, and where the limits are. We'll keep the language human.
Drive Venture is operated by SCOCRAFT LLC. In these Terms, “Drive Venture,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the service and its operator.
1. We're still building this
Drive Venture is an experimental, limited-availability service to help families record supervised driving practice, see licensing progress, and enjoy optional game features. We're working to improve it, but the pilot may change, have errors, experience interruptions, or be discontinued. We'd rather be upfront about that than pretend every feature is perfect.
2. Who can participate
Drive Venture is intended for teen permit holders and their parents or legal guardians. It is not intended for children under 13. A parent or legal guardian must initiate or approve participation for every driver under 18 and is responsible for supervising that minor's use of Drive Venture.
Please provide accurate information and correct material mistakes when you find them. Don't impersonate someone else or use the service for unlawful purposes. The driving log works best when everyone uses their real information.
3. Driving comes first
Never interact with Drive Venture or a mobile phone while driving. Enter or review information only when safely parked. A supervising adult must provide all supervision required by law and should handle any necessary communication during a drive.
Drive Venture is not a driving instructor, real-time vehicle monitor, emergency service, or authority on whether someone is safe or legally permitted to drive. No achievement or progress display changes anyone's legal driving responsibilities.
4. We help with the forms; the state makes the rules
Drive Venture is not a government agency, driving school, insurance company, or legal adviser. We try to reproduce the information and format of state-required supervised-driving logs so families can keep useful records in one place. But our forms, reports, licensing guidance, and progress calculations are informational; they may be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect. A state licensing office or other agency may require a different form, additional proof, stricter documentation, or other steps when your teen applies for a license. Families remain responsible for checking current requirements directly with the appropriate agency and keeping or submitting whatever official logs and certifications it requires. We'd love to make the paperwork simpler. We can't overrule the DMV.
Drive Venture doesn’t want to make anyone’s DMV experience worse: if you find that our formatting is lacking, please consider dropping us a message at https://feedback.mydriveventure.com so that we can make it better for future drivers.
5. Protect your family's account
Your family's Drive Venture account and driving history may contain personal information. Protect your sign-in links and account access, and only share information with people authorized to see it. If you suspect someone has accessed your family's account or records without permission, contact us promptly.
6. Families, licenses, and capacity
Drive Venture gives each Family one place to manage its driving history. A Family's License Holder administers the Family license and purchased capacity. Other authorized grown-ups may help drivers, but that does not automatically make them the License Holder or give them the right to transfer the Family's paid license.

The Free Family experience currently allows one driver, up to two guardians, and five accepted Drive Venture drive records. Once those records are used, you can still see, edit, delete, print, and export existing records; you cannot log additional drives without an approved allowance or a paid Family License. Historical drives entered as individual records count toward the five-drive allowance, while eligible aggregate prior-practice credit does not.

Our initial paid web-beta offer is a **$24.99 USD one-time Family License** with capacity for **three active drivers**. Each **additional active-driver seat costs $9.99 USD as a separate one-time purchase**. Prices are shown before any legally applicable transaction taxes. There is **no subscription and no automatic renewal**. This offer is for U.S. web customers; we will show the applicable price, tax and total before asking you to pay. Eligible Alpha families may have an approved complimentary grandfathered license instead of a charge.

The license belongs to the Family, not an individual driver, phone number, email address, or device. We don't promise that every feature will remain unchanged, that the beta will run forever, or that a purchased entitlement overrides rules for safe and lawful use. A one-time license does not require periodic renewal simply because the beta stage ends.

7. Refunds and billing questions
If a Family License or an additional-driver seat is purchased, the purchaser can request a refund **within seven days of that specific purchase**, provided that **the Family has logged no more than 20 total hours of driving through Drive Venture** at the time of the request. For this limit, we count all logged drive time in the Family, **including drives later deleted**; removing a drive does not reset the 20-hour total. The same seven-day and 20-hour conditions apply to a **$9.99 additional-driver seat**. This is a proposed beta policy pending verified usage-accounting and refund operations. It is not a statement that refunds are currently being processed.

We will also correct erroneous or duplicate charges and honor any refund rights required by applicable law, even if they do not meet our voluntary policy. Drive Venture purchases are one-time charges, so there is no recurring service to cancel. Ending participation or deleting Family records is not itself a refund.

For billing questions or a refund request, use [Feedback](https://feedback.mydriveventure.com) and choose **Billing**. To review your request safely, we may need to verify that you are authorized to act for the Family. Before checkout goes live we will confirm the exact process and provide an appropriate purchase record or reference.

8. Text Parker messages
Text Parker is an optional transactional SMS service for participating pilot families at (320) 372-7537, also known as (320) 3-PARKER.
* Text JOIN to register, get started, and opt in to Text Parker.
* SMS consent is voluntary and separate; it is not a condition of purchase.
* Messages may cover registration, account activity, supervised-drive recording, service status, and requested help.
* Message frequency varies with your use. Message and data rates may apply.
* Reply STOP to opt out. After STOP, only messages necessary to confirm or administer the opt-out may be sent unless you enroll again.
* Reply HELP for help, visit our Help Page, or our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ). If you can’t find what you need there, submit Feedback.
* Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery and availability are not guaranteed.
Each person receiving messages must consent for their own mobile number. A parent or guardian must also authorize a minor driver's participation. Texting is optional, and those choices belong to each participant.
9. Use the service responsibly
You may not interfere with Drive Venture, seek unauthorized access, submit malicious, deceptive, or unlawful content, use automation to overwhelm the service, or use it to harass or harm anyone. These limits help keep the service useful and safe for everyone.
10. Feedback and pilot changes
Feedback is voluntary, and we appreciate it. By providing feedback, you allow us to use it to evaluate and improve Drive Venture without compensation or attribution. We will not publicly identify you without permission.
Because this is a pilot, we may modify, suspend, restrict, or discontinue features. When practical, we will notify participating families of material changes.
11. What belongs to whom
The Drive Venture name, visual identity, Parker character, website content, software, and original materials belong to us. Participating doesn't transfer ownership to you.
Families retain ownership of the information and photographs they submit and give us permission to process those materials only as needed to operate and improve the pilot. Your information stays yours; we need permission to process it so the service can work.
12. Service disclaimers
To the extent permitted by law, Drive Venture is provided “as is” and “as available,” without warranties of uninterrupted availability, accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose, or eligibility for any license or insurance benefit. Nothing in these Terms excludes a warranty or right that cannot legally be excluded.
Put simply, we work to make Drive Venture reliable, but we can't promise the service or its calculations will always be available or accurate.
13. Limits on our liability
To the extent permitted by law, Drive Venture and its operator are not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising from the pilot, loss of records, service interruptions, reliance on progress calculations, or unsafe or unlawful driving conduct. This provision does not limit liability that cannot legally be limited.
14. Leaving the pilot
You may stop participating by contacting us. We may suspend participation for misuse, safety concerns, legal requirements, or pilot administration. Information will be handled according to our Privacy Policy at privacy.mydriveventure.com.
15. Which laws apply
These Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Michigan, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
16. Updates and questions
We may update these Terms by posting a revised version with a new “Last updated” date. When practical, participating families will receive additional notice of material changes. Questions? Send us Feedback at feedback.mydriveventure.com
Pilot legal-review notice: The warranty, liability, governing-law, minor-participation, and intellectual-property provisions should receive professional legal review before a broader commercial launch.

## Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy
What we collect, why we need it, and what you can do about it.
Privacy policies aren't thrilling reading. Still, when a family trusts us with information about a teen driver, the explanation should be clear. Here's what we collect, how we use it, who may receive it, and what choices you have.
Drive Venture is an early pilot for supervised teen-driving practice and licensing progress. It is operated by SCOCRAFT LLC. “Drive Venture,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the service and its operator.
1. Information we collect
What we collect depends on how your family uses Drive Venture:
* Parents and guardians: name, email, relationship to the driver, and mobile number if provided.
* Drivers: name or nickname, email, birthday, licensing stage and dates, home ZIP code, favorite color, optional photo, and mobile number if provided.
* Vehicles: vehicle name, class, color, and other Garage details your family adds—mainly so you and your teen can tell the different cars you drive apart in your records.
* Driving records: dates, start and end times, locations or destinations, vehicle, day and night hours, notes, achievements, and licensing-progress calculations.
* Text Parker: mobile number, consent and opt-out status, message content, timestamps, delivery information, and commands.
* Feedback: comments, ratings, the feature involved, optional contact information, and permission to follow up.
* Service and technical details: internal identifiers; provisioning and migration status; error and audit records; approximate coordinates derived from ZIP code; and limited submission context, which may include device class, operating-system family, browser family and major version, viewport size, language, time zone, referring page, campaign parameters, and public website page views.
* Purchases and billing (when paid checkout is enabled): Family license and capacity purchased, transaction amount and taxes, billing location, payment status, transaction/provider reference, receipt and support records, and evidence of which Terms version the purchaser accepted. The payment provider securely handles card entry.
We do not collect continuous GPS trip tracking. Drive Venture does not receive or store full payment-card numbers or card security codes; a secure payment provider will handle card entry when the web checkout is enabled. Our public pages may use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel for website and advertising measurement. These tools may receive page views, page/referral URLs, campaign parameters, and limited device/browser information. We do not intentionally send names, emails, phone numbers, SMS consent, driver birthdays, drive details, destinations, notes, vehicle details, form field contents, or family records to these measurement services.
We do not create our own unique advertising identifier or use submission context to create a persistent device fingerprint. We use these details to understand the website, not to track an individual family's drives.
3. How we use information
We use information to enroll pilot families; run the driving log and calculate practice time, achievements, possible road conditions, and licensing progress; send requested service and Text Parker messages; help users; secure and troubleshoot the service; prevent duplicate processing; maintain audit records; improve the pilot through feedback and aggregated or de-identified information; measure public outreach and advertising; comply with applicable law; and enforce our Terms.
In other words, we collect information to run the service, support families, and improve what isn't working.
4. Text Parker and mobile information
Text Parker is optional. Consent to receive texts is separate and is not a condition of purchase. Mobile information supports requested transactional messages about registration, account activity, supervised-drive recording, service status, and help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers, SMS consent, or opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose mobile information to service providers and wireless carriers only as needed to operate and deliver messaging, or when required by law.
5. When information is shared
We do not sell personal information. We may share it within the participating family (including the driver and authorizing parent or guardian), with service providers working on our behalf, when reasonably needed to protect users or investigate misuse or satisfy law or valid legal process, and with consent or at the direction of someone authorized to make the request.
6. Website and advertising measurement
Selected public website pages use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure page views and Meta Pixel to measure referrals from advertising and social-media posts. They may process page views, page and referral URLs, campaign parameters, and limited device/browser information. Google Analytics is not installed on authenticated driver application pages or the SMS consent preview; Meta Pixel may run on selected public pages.
We do not intentionally send form contents, names, emails, phone numbers, SMS consent, birthdays, driving details, destinations, notes, vehicles, or family records to Google Analytics or Meta for measurement. Google and Meta process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Drive Venture uses measurement to improve outreach and website experience, not to sell personal information.
We're trying to understand whether our outreach works—not collect a family's driving story for advertising.
7. Service providers
We use outside services to keep Drive Venture running. Here's what they do and what information they may handle:
* Account, application, storage, and email providers: help with sign-in, registration, driving records, family and driver information, consent, feedback, support, and service operations.
* Payment processors: when checkout is enabled, handle secure card entry and payment transactions, and share payment status, amount, necessary billing details, and transaction references with us so we can provide Family licenses, receipts, dispute support, and refunds.
* Text-messaging providers, wireless carriers, and messaging intermediaries: handle the phone numbers, message content, delivery information, consent, and opt-outs needed for Text Parker.
* Website hosting and network providers: handle ordinary requests when you visit public pages. Public website hosting is separate from the storage of family driving records.
* Google Analytics and Meta: handle the limited public-page measurement described above.
* Weather and daylight services: use dates and approximate location coordinates to help calculate weather and daylight conditions, without receiving names or contact information for those calculations.
* Domain and related technical providers: support the operation of our website and related services.
We do not share every category of information with every provider. Providers receive information as needed for the services they perform. The important distinction is what those services do with your information, not the software brand on our servers.
8. How long information stays
* Active registration and driving records are kept while the family participates.
* Inactive pilot registration and driving records are deleted or de-identified within 12 months, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary.
* SMS message content is normally kept for up to 12 months, except when an unresolved support, security, or legal matter requires longer.
* Consent, opt-out, policy-version, provisioning, migration, security, and other audit records may be retained for up to five years after the relevant interaction.
* Provider backups and technical logs follow the providers' normal retention cycles.
Some records have to stay longer to document consent, security, or legal responsibilities; the periods above explain our approach.
9. Access, corrections, and deletion
An adult driver, or a parent or guardian who authorized a minor's participation, may request access, correction, or deletion through [Feedback](https://feedback.mydriveventure.com) (choose **Privacy**). We may verify a request using information we already hold. We aim to complete ordinary verified requests within 30 days.
We may retain minimal records needed to document consent, opt-outs, deletion, security matters, or legal obligations. Information in backups may remain until the provider's normal backup cycle finishes. You can stop Text Parker messages immediately by replying STOP, without waiting for a broader deletion request.
10. Teen drivers and children
Drive Venture is for teen permit holders and their families, not children under 13. A parent or legal guardian must initiate or approve participation for any driver under 18. If we learn we collected personal information directly from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.
11. Security
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards suitable for an early pilot. No service or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We take security seriously, while recognizing that no technology is risk-free.
12. Policy updates
We may revise this policy as the pilot evolves. We will post the revised policy with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when a change materially affects participating families.
13. Questions
Questions about privacy or requests concerning your information? Use [Feedback](https://feedback.mydriveventure.com) and select **Privacy**. If you need help with a charge, choose **Billing**. We'll help you understand what happens with your information.

## Open verification gates

- **Refund instrumentation:** seven days from each purchase, inclusive ≤20 hours combined Family logged driving, including soft-deleted drives and historical time, requires immutable/verified measurement. Eligibility is not to be inferred from currently active drives. Purchased-seat capacity adjustment upon a refund also requires a defined operator rule.
- **Legal/consumer-right exceptions:** retain all legally mandated refund rights; confirm final policy text and sale jurisdiction with the owner.
- **Retention:** owner-provided 12-month inactive/SMS and up-to-five-year audit statements are retained from the source, not independently proven by production automation. Cross-reference BKLG-0204 evidence prior to final approval.
- **Feedback categories:** public form and production database currently accept only legacy categories. The proposed Privacy/Billing options cannot be activated publicly until frontend and backend validation migrate together.
- **Notice timing:** set effective/last-updated dates only at publication; no public Terms/Privacy edit is made by this document.
- **Review provenance:** the owner's document is the wording baseline; changes above are additive commercial/privacy-billing edits and category routing changes, not a claim of verbatim final approval.
