# Draft A — Terms and Conditions

**Effective / Last updated:** [REVIEW: set only at approved publication, preserving historical revision evidence].

### Welcome and who operates Drive Venture

Drive Venture is a web-based supervised-driving practice log for families, with progress tracking and optional game features. It is operated by **SCOCRAFT LLC**. In these Terms, “Drive Venture,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the service and its operator.

These Terms apply to your use of Drive Venture, including its website, family accounts, driving records, and optional Text Parker messaging. We provide the service to help families keep track of practice; we do not provide driving lessons, legal advice, or official licensing approval.

### Families, License Holders, and eligible users

A grown-up establishes and manages a Drive Venture Family. Each Family has one **License Holder**, who administers the Family's license and available driver capacity. Other authorized grown-ups may have access to specific drivers but do not automatically own the Family's commercial rights. A driver may be a member of a family only through the approved family/access model.

Drive Venture is intended for teen learners and their families, not children under 13. A parent or legal guardian must initiate or approve participation for a driver under 18. Families must enter truthful information and maintain appropriate authorization for anyone invited to access their records. Do not share sign-in links or private records with unauthorized people.

### Driving safely

**Never interact with Drive Venture or a phone while driving.** Enter or review records while safely parked. A supervising grown-up is responsible for meeting local supervision and driving laws; Drive Venture does not monitor a vehicle, provide emergency assistance, or establish that anyone is legally eligible to drive.

### Your driving log is a tool, not official certification

Drive Venture records practice you enter, shows progress, and may help produce a printable log. We try to reflect the practice information and formatting families need, but a state or other licensing agency may have **additional or stricter documentation requirements**. Requirements can change, and a practice goal shown for a state without a supported licensing ruleset is **not** a legal requirement or promise of licensing readiness.

You are responsible for verifying the rules that apply to your driver, checking entries, and keeping any records or certifications required by the relevant agency.

### Free and paid Families

A new Free Family currently includes **one driver, up to two guardians, and five accepted Drive Venture drive records**. Detailed historical drives entered into Drive Venture count toward that allowance; eligible aggregate prior-practice credit does not. When the allowance is used, the Family can still view, correct, delete, print, or export its existing log, but cannot add new drives or use Text Parker to create them unless it receives additional capacity or an authorized allowance.

For the initial U.S. paid web beta, a **Drive Venture Family License costs $24.99 USD as a one-time purchase** and includes capacity for **three active drivers**. An extra active-driver seat is a separate **$9.99 USD one-time purchase per seat**, available to paid Families. Prices exclude applicable transaction taxes unless we say otherwise at checkout. There are **no subscriptions, automatic renewals, or recurring cancellation charges** in this offer. Purchase eligibility and payment methods are shown before payment; the paid web beta is United States-only.

Purchases and purchased capacity belong to the Family, not to a particular person, phone number, email, or device. They cannot be transferred between Families except for authorized corrections. Some eligible Alpha Families have a complimentary grandfathered paid entitlement; this is not evidence of a purchase.

A purchased Family License does not expire merely because the paid beta ends. This means the entitlement is intended to continue without a periodic license renewal—not that the service will operate without interruption or be available indefinitely. **[REVIEW: exact service-sunset, material-change and customer-remedy wording before publication.]**

### Purchase confirmation, corrections, and refunds

We disclose the total amount, applicable taxes, relevant license capacity, and material beta limitations before you submit a paid purchase. You will receive purchase or entitlement confirmation after a verified successful transaction. Do not enter payment-card details anywhere except the provider-hosted payment form presented through Drive Venture.

Regardless of any discretionary policy, rights or refunds required by applicable law remain available. Contact us about duplicate or incorrect charges using the support route below. There is no recurring plan to cancel. Ending your participation is separate from any refund request.

### Text Parker messages

Text Parker is optional and uses **(320) 372-7537 / (320) 3-PARKER**. It sends transactional registration, drive, service, and requested-help messages to people who separately opt in for their own phone number; a minor's participation also needs appropriate adult authorization. Consent is **not** a condition of purchase. Message frequency depends on use; message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to opt out or **HELP** for assistance. Carrier delivery is not guaranteed, and carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. See the Privacy Policy for how mobile information is used.

### Sharing, acceptable use, and your submissions

Family members and authorized grown-ups may see information within their granted access. Protect your account and report suspicious access. Do not impersonate others, submit false records, harass people, disrupt our systems, attempt unauthorized access, or use Drive Venture unlawfully.

You retain ownership of information and photographs you submit. You permit us to use them as needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, subject to the Privacy Policy. Drive Venture software, brand elements, and original site content remain the property of their respective owners; a license to use Drive Venture does not transfer those rights. You may voluntarily submit feedback, which we may use to improve Drive Venture without identifying you publicly without permission.

### Beta limits, service changes, and responsibility

Drive Venture is an evolving service. Features may change, have errors, or be temporarily unavailable. We will provide practical notice of material changes where possible. You may stop participating or request removal of family information subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable retention duties. We may restrict or suspend access for misuse, safety, or legal reasons.

To the extent permitted by law, Drive Venture is provided **“as is” and “as available.”** We do not promise uninterrupted service, error-free progress calculations, or eligibility for a license, insurance benefit, or government certification. To the extent permitted by law, Drive Venture and SCOCRAFT LLC are not responsible for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive losses arising from use, interruptions, missing records, or reliance on progress summaries. **Nothing here excludes rights or liability that applicable law does not allow us to exclude.** **[REVIEW: liability, remedies, and state consumer-law wording remain subject to owner approval and later BKLG-0237 professional review.]**

These Terms are governed by Michigan law except where applicable law requires otherwise.

### Questions and changes

For a question, correction, privacy request, or billing concern, start with the **[Drive Venture Feedback form](/feedback/)**. If you are not signed in and need an answer, include a contact email and select the form's follow-up option. For issues the form cannot safely handle, use **[mike@mydriveventure.com](mailto:mike@mydriveventure.com)** as a fallback. For SMS, HELP/STOP remain available directly by text.

# Draft B — Privacy Policy

**Effective / Last updated:** [REVIEW: set only on approval/publication; earlier October 8 date belongs to the current public version].

### What Drive Venture is

Drive Venture is a supervised-driving practice log for families, operated by **SCOCRAFT LLC**. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, with whom we share it, and how families can ask questions or request changes.

### The information we collect

Depending on which features a family uses, we may process:

- **Family and grown-up details:** names, contact emails, family memberships, License Holder and guardian access, and phone numbers if provided.
- **Driver details:** name or nickname, contact details, date of birth, license stage or relevant dates, home ZIP code, preferences, and optional photos or avatar sources.
- **Practice and progress:** driving dates and times, duration and credited night practice, destination or place labels, notes, achievements, prior-practice credit, and licensing-progress calculations.
- **Vehicles:** a vehicle name, class, color, or description supplied to distinguish the cars used for practice.
- **Text Parker:** phone endpoints, person-specific opt-in and opt-out events, message content, delivery events, and drive commands.
- **Feedback and support:** the message, optional name/contact details, category, any screenshots, and follow-up choices.
- **Service/security data:** technical error logs, authentication and access events, records of policy/consent and administrative actions, and limited browser/device, language, time zone, referral, campaign, and submitted-request context.
- **Paid-beta transactions, when checkout is enabled:** purchase and billing references, purchased license/capacity, transaction amount and taxes, payment status, and purchaser billing location needed for receipts, tax review, and support. Card entry occurs in the payment provider's secure form; Drive Venture does **not** receive or store complete payment-card numbers or security codes.

Drive Venture does **not** continuously track a trip with GPS. Some weather/daylight calculations may use approximate location inferred from supplied ZIP code. We do not intentionally create a persistent device fingerprint from submission context.

### Why we use this information

We use information to create and manage Families; authenticate approved people; log and correct drives; calculate practice progress; provide optional Text Parker messaging; answer family requests; provide billing and purchase support; protect the service; investigate errors or abuse; maintain records of consent and transactions; and improve reliability and onboarding. We may use aggregated or de-identified information to understand product use and feedback.

### Family access and teen participation

The Family's License Holder administers the license and family management. Other grown-ups see only the drivers or records they are authorized to access; a person belonging to another Family does not automatically gain access to all of this Family's records. A parent or legal guardian must initiate or approve participation for drivers under 18. Drive Venture is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we received personal information directly from a child under 13, we will review and remove it as appropriate.

### Text Parker and mobile consent

Text Parker participation is optional and separate from purchasing or creating a Family. Each participant must opt in for their own phone endpoint through an approved consent path. Phone numbers, SMS opt-in evidence and message content are used to operate or support requested transactional messaging. Message/data charges may apply; STOP opts out and HELP requests help.

**We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in information, or consent records with unrelated parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.** We share necessary messaging information with delivery providers and carriers, or where law requires it.

### Website and advertising measurement

Selected **public** Drive Venture pages use **Google Analytics** for page-view measurements and **Meta Pixel** to understand traffic from social-media and advertising campaigns. These services may process a page or referral URL, campaign parameters, and limited browser/device information. They are not intended to receive family driving logs, SMS consent, driver birth dates, private notes, phone numbers, email addresses, or form contents. Analytics on public pages is different from processing authenticated family application data. We do not intentionally send private family or driving records to these measurement services.

### When we share information

We may share information:
- Among authorized participants within the Family, based on their access and roles.
- With companies that operate hosting, authentication, storage, communications, payment processing, analytics, security, and support services on our behalf, only for the uses described above.
- To address legitimate security or abuse concerns, comply with law, or respond to lawful process.
- With your direction or permission when the person making the request is authorized.

Ordinary public policy text does not need to enumerate our database service, source-code host, DNS vendor, or every infrastructure supplier. Material processing and advertising measurement remain disclosed; any vendor naming explicitly required by contract or law must be retained.

### How long we keep information

We retain active family records while a Family participates, and keep limited consent, transaction, security, and support evidence where needed to administer rights and legal obligations. Family access/correction/deletion requests can be handled by an authorized operator; deleting a Family may require separate cleanup of private photos and authentication identities and does not automatically cancel or refund a purchase.

Preserve the current promises and their publication history until specifically approved.]**

Provider backups may have a separate deletion cycle. Records needed to show a request, consent, charge, dispute, or legal obligation may outlive other operational data.

### Your choices and requests

An authorized parent/guardian, License Holder, or adult driver can request access, correction, or deletion of information as applicable by using the **[Drive Venture Feedback form](/feedback/)**. For a privacy-related request, choose **“Something else,”** identify that it concerns privacy in the message, provide a contact email if not signed in, and select **“Keep me updated”** so we can respond. We may need to verify your authority before sharing or changing Family information. If the form is not suitable, email **[mike@mydriveventure.com](mailto:mike@mydriveventure.com)**.

We aim to handle ordinary verified requests within 30 days, subject to complexity and applicable rights. STOP directly ends optional Text Parker messages and does not require a general deletion request.

### Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security. We may revise this policy as Drive Venture changes. We will publish the new revision date and provide extra notice if a material change calls for it.
